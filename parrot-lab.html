🦜 Parrot voice lab

Tap Hear it to listen to each parrot voice. Tap ⭐ Use in the game on your favourite and the parrot will talk like that in the game on this computer. Tell Claude the number to make it the parrot's voice everywhere (iPad too).

No sound? Turn the volume up and use Chrome or Safari (the Claude app's built-in browser can't speak). Some voices (Albert, Junior, Fred…) come with Macs and iPads; if a device doesn't have one, that parrot uses the normal voice instead.
